Development Methodology
_ _ _

Our team decided to use Scrum as the development methodology for QueueSmart. Scrum is an Agile framework that divides development into shorter stages called sprints, allowing us to work on different features gradually instead of trying to build the entire system at once.

Scrum is a good fit for our group because we can divide the work into smaller tasks, assign responsibilities among team members, and regularly review our progress along the way.

Since QueueSmart will be built across multiple assignments, Scrum lets us build on what we finish in one stage as we move to the next. As we move from system design to UI/UX, API design, data design, and the final application, we can use what we completed earlier to plan and organize the next part of the project.
